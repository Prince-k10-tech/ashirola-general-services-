Questions

Frequently asked questions

Does Import & Export include clearing and forwarding?

Yes. Clearing and forwarding is part of our import and export service, so one team coordinates the documentation, port clearing and onward movement of your goods.

How do I get a quote?

Send an enquiry through the form on this page, or call us. Tell us what the goods are, where they are coming from or going to, and roughly how much there is. Pricing depends on those details, so we do not publish fixed rates.

What details should I have ready?

A description of the goods, the origin and destination, approximate weight or volume, and any supplier or buyer documents you already have. If you are unsure, send what you have and we will tell you what else is needed.

Do you only handle shipping?

No. Ashiri-Ola also delivers real estate development and general services such as procurement, logistics support and general contracting. The form adapts to whichever you choose.

Where are you based?

Our office is in Ikeja, Lagos State, Nigeria.
