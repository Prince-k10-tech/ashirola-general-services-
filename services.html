Trade & logistics

Import & Export

Includes clearing & forwarding

Importing or exporting goods and clearing and forwarding them are parts of the same journey. Shipments have to be arranged, documents prepared, cargo cleared through customs and the port, and goods moved on to their destination. We coordinate these steps together so you have one team to speak to.

What this covers

  • Sourcing and cross-border movement: support for getting goods from where they are to where they need to be.
  • Documentation: shipping and customs paperwork prepared and checked.
  • Clearing: port clearing and customs processing to get cargo released.
  • Forwarding: haulage and delivery of cleared cargo onward.

What to have ready for a quote

  • A description of the goods
  • Where they are coming from and where they are going
  • Approximate weight or volume
  • Any supplier, buyer or shipping documents you already have

Requirements vary with the type of goods and the route. Send what you have and we will tell you what else is needed.
